Historical claim · failed

The old pointwise “PLV” could not measure phase locking.

|exp(i Δφ)| = 1

That quantity is one for every phase difference. The old phase-slip readout therefore collapsed, and the strongest conductor screenshots are now treated as provenance, not evidence.

What P1 now says

The simple passive mapping is closed.

Across independent recordings, repeated runs, 14 healthy subjects and 14 schizophrenia subjects, the same frozen mapping did not produce reliable incremental prediction.

slow phase direction ≠ useful general predictor here

A slightly negative cross-validated gain is exactly what can happen when an irrelevant extra predictor is added. It is not evidence for “anti-guidance.”

P2 warning

The reverse-direction score needs a new meter.

Healthy h14 produced −0.452; schizophrenia s07 produced −5.079, while cohort medians stay near −0.003.

The relative-MSE score can explode when the baseline error is tiny. That is a numerical fragility, not a biological discovery.

What survives

Traveling waves are still real measurements.

This audit did not test whether cortical traveling waves exist. It tested one particular sensor-space predictive mapping. Real traveling-wave literature, source-aware geometry and state-dependent wave dynamics remain legitimate targets.

The more durable idea from this repo is methodological: structure the claim so that data can kill it.

Next legitimate gate

Use experimental state, not knob hunting.

The S016 EDF+ files contain predefined task/rest annotations. A separate gate was frozen before its result: same bands, same lag, same meter; compare predefined task epochs with rest epochs.

Do not rescue the null by searching frequencies, lags, regions, references or smoothing until something becomes significant.
Interpretation boundary

No quantum-brain claim. No schizophrenia mechanism claim.

“Pilot field” is only a computational nickname. A positive result would have been a difference in a sensor-space predictive screening metric. The observed null does not prove absence of traveling waves, and it does not say anything about consciousness or quantum physics.
